What each column does and doesn't cover

Every case states When (date) and Where (state) — 1087 of 1087. Beyond that, a blank cell means the source record does not state that fact, not that it did not happen:

  • Setting: stated in 1007 of 1087 cases (93%)
  • Drug: stated in 1014 of 1087 cases (93%)
  • Method: stated in 852 of 1087 cases (78%)
  • Prosecution status: stated in 854 of 1087 cases (79%); a documented penalty (fine, settlement, or restitution) exists for 106 of 1087 cases (10%)
  • Role: stated in only 233 of 1087 cases (21%) — filtering or sorting by role silently hides the rest of the registry, which may still describe the same role in prose without a structured field

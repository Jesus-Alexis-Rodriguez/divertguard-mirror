Community Pharmacy / Clinic Drug Diversion Prevention Checklist

Check each item that applies to your program. Use "Not Applicable" where appropriate. Score is calculated in real time.

This checklist scores how completely a controlled substance program documents its controls. There are three versions: hospital, community pharmacy and multi-site health system. Each one is written for the workflows and obligations of that setting.

Every item carries a weight, so the score reflects how much of the program's strength is in place rather than how many boxes are ticked. Read the score this way: above 90% is a strong, well-documented program. Between 70% and 90% means gaps worth scheduling. Below 70% means foundational controls are missing and need attention now.

Work through it with your program team before an audit, not during one. Where a section scores low, the investigation playbook, policy templates and training materials cover the fix. Progress saves in your browser, and results export to PDF or CSV. Section scores may be included anonymously in DivertGuard's benchmarking. No facility, personal or device identifiers are collected.
